Footy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Footy is a free Android app that shows football scores, fixtures and match details. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: Footy doesn't collect, store or share any personal data about you.

What Footy collects

Nothing. Footy has no accounts or sign-up, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer can't see who uses the app or how.

What stays on your phone

Your favourite matches, your preferences (theme, leagues, live updates and alerts) and saved match data are stored only on your phone. They're never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and they're excluded from Android's cloud backups. Uninstalling Footy deletes them.

Where match data comes from

To show scores, fixtures, team logos and news headlines, the app downloads public match information from ESPN's servers (site.api.espn.com and a.espncdn.com). Like any website your phone visits, those servers receive the technical information every internet request carries, such as your IP address. Footy sends them nothing about you: no name, account, location or device identifier. ESPN handles that technical information under its own privacy policy. Footy is not affiliated with ESPN.

News links

When you tap a news headline, the article opens in your own web browser. What happens there is covered by that website's privacy policy, not by Footy.

Notifications

Goal and red-card alerts for your favourite matches are optional. Footy asks for your permission first, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's Preferences or in your phone's settings. Alerts are created on your phone; Footy uses no push-notification service.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download match data and to show when you're offline.
  • Notifications: for the optional goal and red-card alerts.
  • Foreground service: to keep following a favourite live match for alerts while the app is in the background. It shows a visible notification and stops when the match ends, when you tap Stop, or when you close the app.

Children

Footy isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
